About pythcode

A free, phase-based Python learning platform.

What is pythcode?

pythcode teaches Python from zero to intermediate, one phase at a time. Each phase has a short lesson, runnable examples, and 6–10 challenges. Pass the phase test to unlock the next one.

Who is it for?

Complete beginners who want simple English and small steps. Students who want to learn programming at their own pace.

Built with

Python 3 & Flask · SQLite · anime.js & motion.dev · JetBrains Mono · Inter · Syne
